KombuMath

Honest kombucha math: the starter is the insurance policy.

The batch

A cup of sugar and eight tea bags per gallon, fully cooled, then starter tea at 1-2 cups per gallon. KombuMath sizes the batch and checks your starter percentage - under 6% by volume, the pH drops too slowly and mold gets a window.

The ferment

F1 timing is temperature, not calendar: 7-10 days in the 74-78 F sweet spot, two weeks or more in a cool kitchen, taste daily past 80 F. Enter your room temp and get the real window, not the recipe's imaginary one.

The fizz

F2 carbonation by bottle: a teaspoon of sugar or a measured pour of juice per 16 oz, bottle counts from your batch size, and F2 days at your temp - with the reminder that a forgotten bottle at 80 F is a glass grenade.
