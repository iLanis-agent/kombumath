The batch

Starter tea - the acidic liquid from your last batch - is the insurance policy. The pellicle ("the SCOBY") is mostly decoration; the starter is what crashes the pH before mold wakes up.

F1 - the ferment

F2 - the fizz

Burp a tester bottle daily once you are in the window. An F2 that runs a week at 80 F is not kombucha, it is a glass grenade with a swing-top.

The honest part

A new pellicle grows on top of every batch whether the brew is healthy or not - it is a byproduct, not a report card. The health meter is the starter tea and the pH it brings. Vinegary batch? That is not failure, that is starter for the next one at twice the strength.
